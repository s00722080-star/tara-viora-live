TARA VIORA Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 23, 2026

TARA VIORA operates an internal AI-assisted marketing and content management system. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when authorized users connect third-party services, including TikTok.

Information we may process

When an authorized user connects TikTok, we may receive basic account information made available through the permissions the user approves, such as account identifiers and basic profile information. If additional TikTok permissions are approved in the future, we may process the content or publishing data required for those specific features.

How information is used

Information is used only to authenticate the connected account, provide requested integration features, display connection status, and perform actions expressly initiated and approved by an authorized TARA VIORA user.

Storage and security

Integration credentials and tokens are stored server-side with encryption and are not intentionally exposed in the client interface. Access is limited to authorized users of the TARA VIORA system.

Sharing

TARA VIORA does not sell personal information. Data may be transmitted to service providers only as needed to deliver requested functionality, including platform APIs used by the authorized user.

Retention and deletion

Integration data is retained only as needed to operate the connected service or meet legitimate operational and legal requirements. Authorized users may disconnect integrations and request deletion of associated stored credentials and account data.

Your choices

Users control whether to connect a third-party account and which permissions to approve. Permissions can also be revoked through the applicable third-party platform.

Contact

For privacy or data requests related to TARA VIORA, contact the organization administrator responsible for this service.
